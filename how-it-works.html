How it works

Four steps to save a public Instagram video you are allowed to keep.

  1. Open Instagram and copy the link to a public Reel or post (/reel/, /reels/, or /p/).
  2. Paste the URL on the ReelSave downloader.
  3. Click Fetch. Wait for the preview.
  4. Click Download MP4.

What is not supported

  • Private accounts
  • Stories
  • Logged-in-only media
  • Videos you do not have the right to save

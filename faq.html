FAQ

Is ReelSave free?

Yes.

Do I need an Instagram login?

No. Only public Reel and post URLs work.

Why did Fetch fail?

The post may be private, a Story, or Instagram may be blocking anonymous access. The live downloader also needs the ReelSave server running.

May I download anyone’s Reel?

Only if you own it or have permission. Copyright still applies.
